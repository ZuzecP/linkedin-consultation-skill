# Getting the profile in

Three input modes. Offer them in this order and take the best one the user can manage. Whichever
mode is used, you need **text** for the copy and **an image** for the photo and cover.

## Mode 1 — LinkedIn's own PDF export (best available)

> The cleanest option: on your profile, click **More → Save to PDF**. That gives me the full
> text of every section without you having to expand anything. Upload it here, plus one
> screenshot of the top of your profile so I can see your photo and cover image.

Gives complete About and Experience text with no "see more" truncation, all roles, skills,
education and certifications. It is the user's own data export, so nothing is being scraped.

What it does not give: photo and cover quality, the visual impression of the page, the exact
ordering of Top Skills as displayed, or Featured thumbnails. Hence the screenshot alongside it.

## Mode 2 — Full-page screenshot

> Use a browser extension like **GoFullPage** (Chrome) or **Full Page Screen Capture** (Firefox)
> to capture the whole page in one image, exactly as a recruiter or an AI sourcing tool scans it.
> Upload it here. I'll ask for close-ups of specific sections afterwards if I need more detail.

Best for the first-pass assessment, because it shows the profile as it is actually seen. Weakest
for copy work: About and Experience are truncated at "see more".

When you need more, be specific about what you're asking for: "Can you expand your About section
and screenshot it? I want to read the full text before giving feedback."

## Mode 3 — Pasted text

Accept it, and state plainly what cannot be assessed from it: photo, cover, visual impression,
skimmability, section ordering, and whether sections the user didn't paste are empty or just
unmentioned. Ask for a screenshot of the top of the profile to cover the visual half.

## What to capture regardless of mode

- The URL itself, exactly as written, including any trailing number string
- Whether each section exists at all, not just what it says
- Which sections are empty, since absence is a finding
- Whether Open to work is on, and in which mode if visible

## If the profile is not the user's own

The skill is built for self-review. Say so, then continue: all three modes work for a profile
someone has shared with the user. Copy drafted for someone else still needs that person's own
evidence, so the Socratic questioning in `narrative.md` has to reach them somehow, or the copy
stays generic.
